Privacy Policy

Psychotic Lab
Effective date: October 6, 2026

This policy applies to video games published by Psychotic Lab that link to this page. It describes what I do and explains that Microsoft and, where applicable, Unity may handle information through their own services.

Information I collect

I do not personally collect, receive, or store personal information from your gameplay. If you contact me, I will receive the information you choose to include in your message.

Microsoft Store

Microsoft operates the Microsoft Store and may process information when you browse, download, purchase, install, update, or use a game distributed through it. This can include Microsoft account, transaction, device, and diagnostic information, as described in the Microsoft Privacy Statement. Microsoft handles this information under its own policies; I do not access your Microsoft account credentials or payment details through gameplay.

Unity and other third-party services

The games are made with Unity. Unity may process information when Unity services are used or enabled in a particular game; the information and purposes depend on the services and configuration. This policy does not claim that every Unity game automatically sends player information to Unity. See the Unity Privacy Policy for Unity's practices.

A particular game may also include other third-party services or features. Any game-specific privacy notice or information shown in the game or its Store listing should be read alongside this policy.

Children

I do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Microsoft and any third-party service providers may have their own age-related practices and controls; please review their policies.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when the games or their services change. The effective date above shows when it was last revised. For privacy questions, contact me through the repository's issue page. Please do not include sensitive personal information in a public issue.
